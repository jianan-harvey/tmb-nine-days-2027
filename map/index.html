Is the hut on the trail?

Green pins sit within 100 m of the main route. Blue pins are 100–500 m away. Amber pins are farther away or on an alternative. The distance shown in each stay’s card is a straight-line gap to the main track, useful for orientation. It is not the walking length of the access path.

How far to the next hut?

Original directory cards give walking distances published by the TMB association. Added-refuge cards use clearly labelled map estimates where available. The separate main-trail spacing table measures distance along the mapped line. Route variants and local access paths can make these numbers differ.

Expanded on 14 October 2026: the 43 association-directory stays plus 11 additional refuges, including Bonatti, Elena, Elisabetta, Combal, Croix du Bonhomme, Miage, Lac Blanc, Flégère, Bellachat, Col de Balme and Robert Blanc. All pins are numbered counterclockwise from Les Houches. Side-route stays are placed by their nearest point on the main loop. This is a selected set of TMB options, not every mountain hut in the region. Seasonal opening and availability must be checked with each refuge.
