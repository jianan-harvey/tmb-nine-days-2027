Booking strategy

Start with the mountain nights.

  1. Prioritize Bonatti, Mottets, Nant Borrant and Maison Vieille, then connect the Swiss village nights.
  2. Association member bookings open 15 October 2026. Bonatti has its own request process; its 2027 opening date needs confirmation.
  3. Flégère opens requests on 5 January 2027. It does not consider earlier requests.
  4. Ask for available dorm beds in huts and gîtes, including places split across different dorms. If Bonatti is full, try Bertone and Elena and recalculate Days 4–5 before booking. Use hotels only if the hut options do not work.
